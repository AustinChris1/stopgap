# Open Agent Hackathon 2026 — official mechanics (checked 2026-10-20)

Primary page opened this session: the live event page at https://hackathon.genai.works/event/open-agent-hackathon-2026 (HTML saved and read the same day; registration counter on that copy was 4,911). Where the GenAI Academy course page disagrees with that event page, both are quoted and the disagreement is marked. Secondary pages are labeled as such. Pages that did not return body text are not used as evidence.

## What does the event page say today about dates, sponsors, tracks, prizes, eligibility, submission, and judging?

### Takeaway
As of 2026-10-20 the public event page is a six-day online hackathon, 22–27 October 2026 UTC, teams of 1–5, open to age 16+, prize pool stated as up to $20,000, scored out of 100 with Impact at 30. The same page’s rules block is more specific than the hero: $13,000 cash plus $7,000 in-kind, and every submission must integrate Zetaris and Meterless and use Cursor.

### Inferences
- Cash on the event-page cards sums to $13,000 (3,700 + 3,700 + 3,600 + 2,000) and the two in-kind figures sum to $7,000 (4,699 + 2,301), which matches rules section 08. That arithmetic is a check on the page, not a separate published total.
- NVIDIA is worded as “integrate wherever it contributes,” which is weaker than the mandatory “must integrate Zetaris and Meterless” and “must use Cursor.” The page does not say a submission is invalid without NVIDIA.
- Registration stays open for almost 24 hours after the build window opens (00:00 vs 23:59 UTC on 22 October). The page does not say late registrants get extra build time.

### Gaps
- The event page says Impact is “consistency with the challenge statements,” but the only challenge text on that page is the one-sentence track blurbs above. No longer challenge statement, dataset, or success test is in the page HTML.
- “Judging sponsors,” “sponsor-track,” and whether in-kind awards can stack with cash places are not defined.
- No per-judge rubric, no statement that judges will try to break the agent, and no HackOS field list appear on this page.
